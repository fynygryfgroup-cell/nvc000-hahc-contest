The COA submittals for 1505 Tulane Street claim a proposed Floor Area Ratio (FAR) of 3,450 square feet — just 70 square feet below the 40% maximum on the combined 8,799 sq ft lot. With so little margin, any single arithmetic error or improper exemption is sufficient to push the project over the legal cap. The submittals contain at least three separate FAR problems — each independent of the others — plus an internal contradiction on the existing home's square footage that undermines the reliability of all declared metrics. Taken together, these discrepancies indicate that the true FAR significantly exceeds the 3,450 sq ft declared.

## Section V.B — Floor Area Ratio (FAR) Discrepancies

**Claimed FAR:** The submittals claim a proposed FAR of 3,450 sq ft (39.21%) against a maximum allowable of 3,520 sq ft (40%) on the 8,799 sq ft lot — a buffer of only **70 sq ft** [15][16].

---

### Problem 1 — The Detached Garage Exemption Is Invalid: The Garage Is Attached

The submittal deducts **528 sq ft** for a "detached" garage from the FAR/lot coverage calculations. The garage's first-floor footprint is stated as 680 sq ft [17]. The narrative claims the garage is "connected to the main house only by an unconditioned breezeway" [17].

<Warning>
**MAJOR FINDING — The "breezeway" is actually an enclosed, conditioned living-space connection.** Upon visual inspection of the proposed second floor plan (A01, Sheet 19), the connection between the main house and the garage apartment is not an unconditioned breezeway. It is a fully enclosed, conditioned **GALLERY** measuring **15′-5″ × 4′-5″**, with finished wood flooring, exterior walls, windows, and interior doors. Adjacent to this gallery is an **ELEVATOR** shaft measuring **5′-4″ × 4′-2″**. This is habitable, conditioned floor area physically connecting the main house second floor directly to the garage apartment.
</Warning>

This means:

- The garage is **attached**, not detached — the 528 sq ft exemption is forfeited.
- The gallery and elevator square footage (approximately 68 sq ft + 22 sq ft = **~90 sq ft** of connection space) may be uncounted conditioned floor area not included in the FAR calculation.
- The roof plan (A01, Sheet 20) confirms the garage and breezeway are under a continuous roof plane connecting directly to the main house.
- If the garage is reclassified as attached, the project FAR jumps to approximately **3,978 sq ft (45.2%)** — exceeding the strict 40% legal cap.

---

### Problem 1b — Garage Footprint Dimensions Don't Match the Claimed Square Footage

<Warning>
**MAJOR FINDING — 196 sq ft garage footprint discrepancy.** The proposed site plan drawing (A01, Sheet 17) dimensions the garage footprint as **21′-1″ × 22′-11½″**. Multiplying these dimensions yields approximately **484 sq ft** — yet the submittal narrative claims a **680 sq ft** first-floor footprint. This is a **196 sq ft discrepancy** (approximately 40% more than the drawn dimensions support). Either the drawings are wrong or the claimed square footage is inflated. The garage size appears to be "in flux" between what is drawn and what is claimed.
</Warning>

| Measurement | Value |
|---|---|
| Garage dimensions on site plan (A01, Sheet 17) | 21′-1″ × 22′-11½″ |
| Calculated footprint from drawn dimensions | ~484 sq ft |
| Claimed footprint in narrative | 680 sq ft |
| **Discrepancy** | **196 sq ft (~40% more than drawn)** |

---

### Problem 2 — Arithmetic Error in the Lot Coverage Table

The lot coverage schedule lists the detached garage (over 528 sq ft) as **149 sq ft** [18][19]. The correct figure should be 680 − 528 = **152 sq ft**. This 3 sq ft discrepancy, while small, indicates a basic arithmetic error in the official COA forms and calls into question the reliability of all calculations in the packet.

| Item | Claimed | Correct Calculation | Error |
|---|---|---|---|
| Garage footprint | 680 sq ft | — | — |
| Less: detached exemption | 528 sq ft | — | — |
| Net lot coverage contribution | **149 sq ft** | **152 sq ft** | **3 sq ft arithmetic error** |

---

### Problem 3 — Internal Floor Area vs. Declared FAR

Summing the individual room dimensions on the floor plans (Living, Dining, Kitchen, Mudroom, Master Suite, Family Room) yields approximately **3,312 sq ft** for the main house alone. Adding the second-floor garage apartment (649 sq ft) brings the total to approximately **3,961 sq ft** — over **500 sq ft more** than the 3,450 sq ft total FAR declared on the submittal. The applicant appears to have understated the actual floor area.

| Component | Square Footage |
|---|---|
| Main house (room dimensions summed) | ~3,312 sq ft |
| Second-floor garage apartment | 649 sq ft |
| **Total actual floor area (estimated)** | **~3,961 sq ft** |
| Declared FAR in submittal | 3,450 sq ft |
| **Understatement** | **~511 sq ft** |
| Declared FAR as % of lot | 39.21% |
| **Actual FAR as % of lot (estimated)** | **~45.0%** |

## Section V.E — Existing Home Square Footage Contradiction: 2,192 vs. 1,299 sq ft

<Warning>
**MAJOR FINDING — Internal square footage contradiction.** The A01 submittal contains an internal contradiction on page 1: it states both that the property is a "2,192 square foot" residence [26] and that "the existing home is 1,299 sq ft" [27]. These two figures, appearing on the **same page**, cannot both be correct. The **893 sq ft difference** is material to the FAR calculation and must be reconciled before the Commission can rely on the submitted metrics.
</Warning>

| Reference | Stated Existing Square Footage | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Description of property | 2,192 sq ft | A01, p.1, ref. [26] |
| Existing home statement | 1,299 sq ft | A01, p.1, ref. [27] |
| **Discrepancy** | **893 sq ft** | — |

This internal contradiction is significant because the existing home's square footage is the baseline for the entire FAR calculation. If the "1,299 sq ft" figure is correct, the addition is substantially larger than disclosed. If the "2,192 sq ft" figure is correct, the FAR calculation understates the existing home's contribution to the total. Either way, the declared FAR of 3,450 sq ft cannot be verified.

## Section I.H — The 25% Encroachment Line

<Warning>
**MAJOR FINDING — The second floor plan explicitly labels a "25% ENCROACHMENT" line.** Upon visual inspection of the proposed second floor plan (A01, Sheet 19), a dashed line is explicitly labeled **"25% ENCROACHMENT"** running across the floor plan. The garage/breezeway area is annotated with the note: *"GARAGE/BREEZEWAY FLOOR PLAN: 25% ENCROACHMENT NEW CONSTRUCTION APPLICATION."*
</Warning>

This notation appears to be an acknowledgment that the second-floor massing encroaches into a restricted area — likely the lot coverage or massing envelope limit. The HAHC should require the applicant to explain exactly what this encroachment line represents and how the proposed second floor complies with the Heights Design Guidelines massing limits when it self-identifies as encroaching.

## Summary of FAR Problems

| Problem | Nature | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Detached garage exemption | Garage is structurally attached; exemption is invalid | +528 sq ft to FAR → ~3,978 sq ft (45.2%) |
| Garage footprint discrepancy | Drawn 484 sq ft vs. claimed 680 sq ft | 196 sq ft discrepancy — drawings or narrative wrong |
| Lot coverage arithmetic error | 149 vs. 152 sq ft | 3 sq ft error in official COA forms |
| Understated floor area | ~3,961 sq ft actual vs. 3,450 sq ft declared | ~511 sq ft understatement |
| Existing home contradiction | 2,192 vs. 1,299 sq ft on same page | Baseline FAR figure unverifiable |
| 25% Encroachment label | Self-identified encroachment on second floor plan | Requires explanation from applicant |

The project as submitted cannot be approved without reconciliation of these discrepancies. The declared FAR buffer of only 70 square feet is illusory given the multiple identified sources of undercounting.
