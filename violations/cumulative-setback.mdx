The Houston Heights Historic District Design Guidelines impose a cumulative side setback requirement that is separate from, and additive to, the per-side minimum. For two-story houses, the cumulative total of both side setbacks must reach at least 15 feet. The applicant's submitted site plan shows a north (plat "East") side setback of 3′-6½″ and a south (plat "West") side setback of 8′-5 15/16″ — totaling approximately 12 feet cumulative. This falls 3 feet short of the mandatory 15-foot minimum, and represents a direct, face-of-drawing violation that is independent of the boundary dispute.

## Section V.C — NEW FINDING: Cumulative Side Setback Violation

### The Design Guidelines Requirement

The Houston Heights Design Guidelines, Section 5 (February 2018), explicitly state:

> *"Within the Houston Heights Historic Districts, the side setback is increased to a minimum of five feet on each side and a cumulative total of 10 feet for one-story houses and **15 feet for two-story houses.**"* [22]

The applicant's proposed two-story addition and two-story garage apartment are both two-story structures. The Design Guidelines therefore require a minimum cumulative side setback of **15 feet** for the two-story portions.

### Cumulative Setback Calculation

The submitted site plan drawing (A01, Sheet 17) shows the following side setbacks:

| Side | Plat Label | Real-World Direction | Drawn Dimension |
|---|---|---|---|
| North side | Plat "East" | North (toward 1507 Tulane) | 3′-6½″ |
| South side | Plat "West" | South (toward 1501 Tulane) | 8′-5 15/16″ |
| **Cumulative total** | — | — | **~12′-0″** |

The following table shows the full compliance picture:

| Scenario | North Side | South Side | Cumulative | Required | Deficiency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **As drawn** | 3′-6½″ | 8′-5 15/16″ | ~12′-0″ | 15′-0″ | **–3′-0″** |
| If corrected to 5′ min. (north) | 5′-0″ | 8′-5 15/16″ | ~13′-5 15/16″ | 15′-0″ | **–1′-6⅛″** |

Even if the north (plat "East") side setback were corrected to the mandatory 5-foot minimum, the cumulative total would be only **13′-5 15/16″** — still **1′-6⅛″ below the 15-foot requirement**. The project as designed cannot meet the cumulative side setback standard for two-story construction in the Houston Heights Historic District.

## Exhibit I — Houston Heights Design Guidelines: Cumulative Side Setback Rule

### The Controlling Provision

The Houston Heights Design Guidelines, Section 5 (February 2018), explicitly state:

> *"Within the Houston Heights Historic Districts, the side setback is increased to a minimum of five feet on each side and a cumulative total of 10 feet for one-story houses and 15 feet for two-story houses."* [1]

### Additional Design Guidelines Provisions

The following additional provisions from Section 5 of the Houston Heights Design Guidelines apply to this project:

**Existing house less than 5′ from property line:**

> If the existing house is less than five feet from the property line, a one-story addition can match the existing side setback or a three-foot side setback, whichever is greater. A two-story addition must have a minimum five-foot setback. [2]

**Eave height determination:**

> For the purpose of determining maximum allowable eave height, the side setback for the entire building is measured at the portion of the building that is closest to the property line. [2]

**Rear setback:**

> The City of Houston requires a minimum setback of three feet from the rear property line, except that a front-facing garage located with its rear wall at the alley may have a zero-foot setback. [3]

**Alley-loading garage clearance:**

> An alley-loading garage generally must establish a minimum of 20 feet of clearance from an opposing alley-loading garage door, the rear wall of a front-facing garage, or a fence; a 24-foot clearance is preferred. [4]

**Lot coverage — detached garages:**

> Detached garages up to 400 square feet are excluded from lot coverage calculations; detached garages over 400 square feet are included. [5][6]

> Pavement and driveways are excluded from lot coverage. [6]

### Application to the Proposed Project

The proposed addition and garage apartment are both two-story structures. The Design Guidelines therefore require a minimum cumulative side setback of **15 feet**. The submitted site plan shows:

| Side | Plat Label | Drawn Dimension |
|---|---|---|
| North (plat "East") | East | 3′-6½″ |
| South (plat "West") | West | 8′-5 15/16″ |
| **Cumulative** | — | **~12′-0″** |

This is a **face-of-drawing violation** of the Houston Heights Design Guidelines cumulative side setback requirement for two-story houses. The cumulative side setback of approximately 12 feet falls **3 feet short** of the mandatory 15-foot minimum.

## Independence from the Boundary Dispute

<Info>
This violation does **not** depend on the boundary dispute. It is apparent on the face of the submitted drawings using the applicant's own dimensions. Even if both side setbacks were measured from the applicant's claimed property lines (including the disputed southern line), the cumulative total still fails to reach 15 feet.
</Info>

The HAHC should require the applicant to demonstrate compliance with the 15-foot cumulative side setback minimum for two-story houses. The current drawings show approximately 12 feet cumulative — a direct, face-of-drawing violation of the Houston Heights Design Guidelines.
