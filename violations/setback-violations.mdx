The proposed site plan drawing for 1505 Tulane Street contains face-of-drawing setback violations that are independent of the ongoing boundary dispute. The violations documented in this section are visible on the face of the applicant's own submitted drawings — no boundary adjustment is required to identify them. The north (plat "East") side wall is drawn inside the minimum building line the applicant themselves annotated, and the rear setback shown on the drawing is 23 feet less than what the narrative text claims. These are not measurement disputes; they are direct contradictions within the applicant's own submission.

## Section V.C — Setback Compliance Issues (Chapter 33)

### Claimed Setbacks

The submittal narratives state a east (plat "South") side setback of 20', a west (plat "North") side setback of 7', and a cumulative side setback of 27' [20]. The rear setback is stated as "24' at least" [21].

The following table compares the narrative claims against the dimensions shown on the actual submitted site plan drawing (A01, Sheet 17):

| Setback | Claimed (Narrative) | Drawn (Site Plan, A01 Sheet 17) | Discrepancy |
|---|---|---|---|
| North (plat "East") side setback | Not specified in narrative | **3′-6½″** | Below 5′ minimum by 1′-5½″ |
| East (plat "South") side setback | 20′ | — | — |
| West (plat "North") side setback | 7′ | — | — |
| West/Rear (plat "North") setback | **"24′ at least"** | **1′-0⅛″** | **23-foot discrepancy** |
| Cumulative side setback | 27′ | ~12′ | 15-foot shortfall vs. claimed; 3-foot shortfall vs. required |

### North Side Setback Violation

The north (plat "East") side setback is a direct, face-of-drawing violation. The proposed site plan shows the addition's north (plat "East") wall at **3′-6½″** from the north (plat "East") property line — **1′-5½″ below the mandatory 5′ minimum side setback** required under Chapter 33. This is not dependent on the boundary dispute; it is a violation on the face of the submitted drawing. The drawing itself shows a "5' SIDE B.L." (building line) marker, and the wall is drawn inside it.

### West/Rear Setback Discrepancy

The west/rear (plat "North") setback discrepancy is extreme. The narrative claims "24' at least" for the rear setback, but the drawing shows the addition at **1′-0⅛″** from the west (plat "North") property line. This **23-foot discrepancy** is not a rounding error — it suggests either a fundamental error in the submittal or a deliberate misrepresentation of the rear setback in the narrative to mask what the drawings actually show.

### The 5-Foot Minimum Problem (Boundary Dispute Interaction)

The minimum single-side setback in the Houston Heights Historic District is 5′. The applicant claims a 7′ west (plat "North") side setback. If the boundary dispute along the southern line reveals that the actual property line sits even 2′ closer than the applicant's plan claims, the setback drops below the mandatory 5′ minimum, triggering an immediate violation of the Historic Preservation ordinance.

### Fire Code Impact

Under the City of Houston Building Code (IRC/IBC), any exterior wall located less than 5 feet from a property line must meet strict fire-resistance rating standards:

- **Less than 5′ from property line:** 1-hour fire-rated construction required (tested per ASTM E119/UL 263) from both sides.
- **Less than 3′ from property line:** Generally 0% openings permitted — no unprotected windows or doors.

The submitted elevation drawings show **standard wall construction and window placements** along the north (plat "East") elevation. If this wall is within 3′-6½″ of the property line as drawn, the drawings fail to incorporate the required fire-rated assemblies and may show prohibited openings. The Permitting Office should flag this during plan review.

This issue is compounded by the boundary dispute: if the true property line is even closer than the applicant claims, the fire separation distance shrinks further, increasing the fire-rating requirements. **The north (plat "East") wall at 3′-6½″ is already within this fire code trigger zone per the applicant's own drawing.**

## Independence from the Boundary Dispute

<Info>
The setback violations documented on this page do **not** depend on the resolution of the boundary dispute between 1501 and 1505 Tulane Street. They are visible on the face of the submitted drawings using the applicant's own dimensions and the applicant's own annotated building line markers. Even if the boundary dispute were resolved entirely in the applicant's favor, these violations would remain.
</Info>

The north (plat "East") side wall violation and the west/rear setback discrepancy are:

1. **Internally inconsistent** — the narrative and the drawing disagree by 23 feet on the rear setback alone.
2. **Independently violative** — the north wall is drawn inside the "5' SIDE B.L." marker the applicant placed on their own drawing.
3. **Fire code triggering** — the 3′-6½″ dimension places the wall in the mandatory 1-hour fire-rated construction zone under IRC Table R302.1.

The HAHC and the Permitting Office should require the applicant to reconcile the narrative claims with the actual site plan dimensions before these applications are reconsidered.
