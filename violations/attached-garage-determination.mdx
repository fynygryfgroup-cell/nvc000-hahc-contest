The COA submittals claim a 528 square foot FAR exemption for a "detached" garage, on the basis that the garage is "connected to the main house only by an unconditioned breezeway." The second floor plan, the roof plan, and the foundation evidence together refute this characterization completely. The connection between the main house and the garage is an enclosed, conditioned gallery with finished wood flooring, exterior walls, windows, and interior doors — adjacent to a fully enclosed elevator shaft. The roof plan shows no structural gap between the garage and the main house. These are not the features of a detached garage. They are the features of a single, contiguous, attached structure. The 528 sq ft exemption is forfeited, and when it is removed, the project FAR jumps to approximately 3,978 sq ft — 45.2% of the lot area, exceeding the strict 40% legal cap by more than 5 percentage points.

## Section V.B — Problem 1: The Detached Garage Exemption Is Invalid

**Claimed:** The submittal deducts **528 sq ft** for a "detached" garage from the FAR/lot coverage calculations. The garage's first-floor footprint is stated as 680 sq ft [17]. The narrative claims the garage is "connected to the main house only by an unconditioned breezeway" [17].

<Warning>
**MAJOR FINDING — The "breezeway" is actually an enclosed, conditioned living-space connection.** Upon visual inspection of the proposed second floor plan (A01, Sheet 19), the connection between the main house and the garage apartment is not an unconditioned breezeway. It is a fully enclosed, conditioned **GALLERY** measuring **15′-5″ × 4′-5″**, with finished wood flooring, exterior walls, windows, and interior doors. Adjacent to this gallery is an **ELEVATOR** shaft measuring **5′-4″ × 4′-2″**. This is habitable, conditioned floor area physically connecting the main house second floor directly to the garage apartment.
</Warning>

### The Gallery and Elevator Connection

The connection between the main house and the garage apartment contains two distinct enclosed elements:

| Element | Dimensions | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Gallery | 15′-5″ × 4′-5″ (~68 sq ft) | Fully enclosed; conditioned; finished wood flooring; exterior walls; windows; interior doors |
| Elevator shaft | 5′-4″ × 4′-2″ (~22 sq ft) | Enclosed elevator providing vertical access between floors |
| **Total connection footprint** | — | **~90 sq ft of conditioned, enclosed connection space** |

This means:

- The garage is **attached**, not detached — the **528 sq ft exemption is forfeited**.
- The gallery and elevator square footage (~90 sq ft) may be **uncounted conditioned floor area** not included in the declared FAR.
- The roof plan (A01, Sheet 20) confirms the garage and breezeway are under a **continuous roof plane** connecting directly to the main house.
- If the garage is reclassified as attached, the project FAR jumps to approximately **3,978 sq ft (45.2%)** — exceeding the strict 40% legal cap.

## Section V.H — Continuous Roofline and Foundation Connection

<Warning>
**MAJOR FINDING — The roof plan shows a continuous roof connection.** Upon visual inspection of the proposed roof plan (A01, Sheet 20), the garage and breezeway are shown under a continuous roof plane that connects directly to the main house addition. While the garage has its own distinct hipped roof mass, the connector/breezeway roof ties directly into the exterior wall and lower eaves of the two-story addition. This is not a gap or a separate structure — it is a **structurally connected roofline**.
</Warning>

### Roof Plan Evidence

Upon visual inspection of the proposed roof plan (A01, Sheet 20), the garage and breezeway are shown under a continuous roof plane that connects directly to the main house addition. While the garage has its own distinct hipped roof mass, the connector/breezeway roof ties directly into the exterior wall and lower eaves of the two-story addition. **This is not a gap or a separate structure — it is a structurally connected roofline.**

### Foundation Implications

The submittal packets do not include a dedicated foundation plan showing whether the garage and main house share a continuous slab or footing. However, the combination of:

1. A **continuous roofline**,
2. An **enclosed conditioned gallery with an elevator** connecting the second floors, and
3. The **absence of any structural separation joint**

strongly indicates the garage and house are being built as a **single contiguous structure**.

### Code Impact

Under Houston Permitting standards, a continuous roofline and continuous foundation footings connecting structures convert a "detached" garage into an **attached structure**. This:

- **Forfeits the 528 sq ft detached garage FAR exemption** (pushing FAR over the 40% cap).
- Means the garage **cannot be treated as a separate accessory structure** for setback purposes.
- If the structure spans across the interior lot line between Lots 16 and 17 without a recorded replat or binding agreement, it constitutes an **illegal building across an un-replatted lot line** under Chapter 42.

## Section V.I — "25% Encroachment" Line on Second Floor Plan

<Warning>
**MAJOR FINDING — The second floor plan explicitly labels a "25% ENCROACHMENT" line.** Upon visual inspection of the proposed second floor plan (A01, Sheet 19), a dashed line is explicitly labeled **"25% ENCROACHMENT"** running across the floor plan. The garage/breezeway area is annotated with the note: *"GARAGE/BREEZEWAY FLOOR PLAN: 25% ENCROACHMENT NEW CONSTRUCTION APPLICATION."*
</Warning>

This notation appears to be an acknowledgment that the second-floor massing encroaches into a restricted area — likely the lot coverage or massing envelope limit. The HAHC should require the applicant to explain exactly what this encroachment line represents and how the proposed second floor complies with the Heights Design Guidelines massing limits when it self-identifies as encroaching.

## Conversion of Exterior Space to Interior Conditioned Space

The plans reveal a pattern of converting previously open, exterior floor area (decks, porches, open breezeways) into enclosed, air-conditioned square footage:

- The **historic rear porch/utility area** (visible on the 1925 Sanborn / 1937 Tax Card) has been absorbed into primary conditioned space (Mudroom, Family Room, Gallery) without clearly delineating the historic rear wall line.
- The **unconditioned breezeway** is calculated as open space for the FAR exemption, yet the elevation drawings indicate full roof framing ties and continuous foundation footings connecting the main home to the garage.
- **Space previously designated as open deck/breezeway** has been enclosed into conditioned living space (elevator, gallery connector, additional bath/closet areas).

This pattern of enclosure pushes the building footprint to the maximum property setbacks and directly conflicts with the historic preservation lot coverage boundaries.

## Structural Attachment: Three Independent Lines of Evidence

The garage is attached — not detached — by three independent and mutually reinforcing lines of evidence:

<Steps>

### Second Floor Plan (A01, Sheet 19) — Enclosed, Conditioned Gallery
The connection is a **fully enclosed, conditioned GALLERY** (15′-5″ × 4′-5″) with finished wood flooring, exterior walls, windows, and interior doors — not an unconditioned breezeway. Adjacent to it is an **ELEVATOR** shaft (5′-4″ × 4′-2″) providing vertical access between floors.

### Roof Plan (A01, Sheet 20) — Continuous Roofline
The garage and breezeway are shown under a **continuous roof plane** connecting directly to the main house addition. The connector/breezeway roof ties directly into the exterior wall and lower eaves of the two-story addition. No gap or structural separation joint is shown.

### Foundation Evidence — Absence of Structural Separation
The submittal packets do not include a dedicated foundation plan. The combination of a continuous roofline and an enclosed conditioned gallery with no shown structural separation joint strongly indicates the structures are built as a **single contiguous structure** — meeting the Houston Permitting standard for an attached structure.

</Steps>

---

## HAHC and Permitting Office Action Requested

The HAHC and the Permitting Office should:

1. **Reclassify the garage as attached**, consistent with the second floor plan, roof plan, and absence of structural separation evidence.
2. **Require corrected FAR calculations** reflecting the forfeiture of the 528 sq ft detached garage exemption — producing a total FAR of approximately 3,978 sq ft (45.2%), exceeding the 40% cap.
3. **Require the applicant to explain the "25% ENCROACHMENT" notation** on the second floor plan and demonstrate how the project complies with massing envelope limits.
4. **Require foundation plans** showing whether the garage and main house share continuous footings — a prerequisite for determining whether the structure constitutes an illegal build across the un-replatted interior lot line between Lots 16 and 17.

<Info>
The attached garage determination interacts with several other findings in this opposition record. A reclassified attached garage (1) forfeits the FAR exemption, pushing FAR over the 40% cap; (2) cannot be treated as a separate accessory structure for setback purposes; and (3) if spanning the interior lot line, constitutes an illegal build across an un-replatted lot line under Chapter 42.
</Info>
