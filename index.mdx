This record constitutes the formal administrative opposition filed by the adjacent property owners at 1501 Tulane Street — represented by Anthony Leavitt & LEAVITTHAAS LLC — against two Certificate of Appropriateness applications for the neighboring property at 1505 Tulane Street, Houston Heights West Historic District. The opposition was prepared in connection with proceedings before the Houston Archaeological and Historical Commission (HAHC) and the City of Houston Permitting Office, and is also shared with legal counsel. It documents an active boundary dispute, an unapproved lot combination, multiple face-of-drawing code violations, and material misrepresentations in the applicant's COA submittals.

<Note>
  **COA Application Numbers and Hearing Timeline**

  This opposition record addresses two Certificate of Appropriateness applications filed for 1505 Tulane Street, Lots 16 & 17, Block 145, Houston Heights:

  | Application | File Number | Scope |
  |---|---|---|
  | Garage & Apartment | **HP2026-0244** | Detached garage with second-floor apartment (Architectural Set A02) |
  | Main House Addition & Rehabilitation | **HP2026-0248** | Main house addition and rehabilitation (Architectural Set A01) |

  At the **September 10, 2026** HAHC hearing, the Commission deferred both applications after the adjacent property owners at 1501 Tulane Street raised an active boundary dispute on the shared southern property line. This opposition record was prepared and filed on **September 24, 2026**, consolidating the full factual and regulatory record in support of a request that the Commission deny or continue to defer both applications.

  The report was prepared by Anthony Leavitt & LEAVITTHAAS LLC on behalf of the property owners at 1501 Tulane Street, whose family has owned and occupied the home continuously since 2009 — and whose family members owned it from 2000 to 2009 — a combined occupancy of over 25 years.
</Note>
