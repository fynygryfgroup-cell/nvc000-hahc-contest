Because no recorded Replat or Agreement to Bind Properties exists for Lots 16 and 17 at 1505 Tulane Street, each lot must independently satisfy the 40% maximum Floor Area Ratio requirement under the Houston Heights Design Guidelines and City of Houston Chapter 33. When that analysis is performed — as the law requires — the project fails on both lots simultaneously, for distinct but equally disqualifying reasons.

## Section III.E-2 — Per-Lot FAR Calculations: The Project Fails on Both Lots Independently

If Lots 16 and 17 remain legally separate (the current state of the record), each lot must independently satisfy the 40% maximum FAR requirement under the Houston Heights Design Guidelines and City of Houston Chapter 33. The combined 8,799 sq ft lot area splits into two approximately equal lots of ~4,400 sq ft each.

---

### North Lot — Main House + Addition

The north lot alone — containing the main house and proposed two-story addition — has a FAR that far exceeds the 40% maximum permitted on a single ~4,400 sq ft lot. Even using the applicant's most conservative claimed figure (1,299 sq ft existing + addition), the total would approach or exceed the 1,760 sq ft per-lot cap. Using the more realistic floor plan room sums (~3,300+ sq ft), the north lot FAR is nearly double the legal maximum.

### South Lot — Garage + Garage Apartment

| Metric | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| South lot area (approx.) | ~4,400 sq ft | Half of combined 8,799 sq ft parcel |
| 40% FAR cap (per-lot) | ~1,760 sq ft | Maximum allowable on ~4,400 sq ft lot |
| Garage footprint (drawn dimensions) | ~484 sq ft | Per site plan drawing: 21'-1" × 22'-11½" |
| Garage footprint (narrative claim) | 680 sq ft | Per A02, p.1 — 196 sq ft discrepancy vs. drawn |
| Second-floor garage apartment | ~649 sq ft | Per submittal |
| Total south lot structure (drawn) | ~1,133 sq ft | Garage + apartment (using drawn dimensions) |
| Total south lot structure (narrative) | ~1,000–1,133 sq ft | Within FAR cap numerically |
| Main residence on south lot? | **None** | Garage and apartment are accessory structures only |

The south lot's garage and garage apartment total approximately 1,000–1,133 sq ft — numerically under the 1,760 sq ft per-lot FAR cap. However, the south lot has a more fundamental problem: **a garage and garage apartment are accessory structures, and an accessory structure cannot be the primary structure on a lot that has no main residence.**

<Warning>
**South Lot Impermissible Configuration:** Under City of Houston ordinances, an accessory structure is only permitted in conjunction with a main building on the same lot. If the garage sits on the south lot without a main residence, it is an impermissible configuration — regardless of whether the FAR number is within limits. The project fails on the south lot not because of a numbers violation, but because of a fundamental configuration violation that cannot be cured without either (a) establishing a main residence on the south lot, or (b) legally merging the two lots through a recorded replat.
</Warning>

---

### Conclusion

When evaluated on a per-lot basis (as required without a recorded replat), the project fails on the north lot due to FAR exceeding the 40% maximum, and fails on the south lot because an accessory structure cannot serve as the primary structure on a lot without a main residence. The project is only numerically feasible by combining both lots — which has not been legally accomplished.

| Lot | Structure | Per-Lot FAR Result | Configuration Result | Overall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **North Lot** | Main house + two-story addition | **FAILS** — approaches or far exceeds 1,760 sq ft cap | House on lot: permissible in principle | **Non-compliant** |
| **South Lot** | Garage + garage apartment | Numerically within cap (~1,000–1,133 sq ft) | **FAILS** — no main residence; accessory structure without primary use | **Non-compliant** |
| **Combined (as submitted)** | Entire project across 8,799 sq ft | 3,450 sq ft claimed (39.21%) | Requires recorded lot-joining instrument — not on record | **Legally invalid basis** |

## Section V.B (P-6) — Impervious Cover Calculation May Be Affected by Boundary Dispute

The submittal calculates total impervious coverage at 4,453 sq ft (50.61%) against a 70% maximum on the 8,799 sq ft lot [38]. However, if the boundary dispute reduces the actual lot area (by moving the southern line north to its monumented position), the lot area decreases and the impervious cover percentage increases.

<Warning>
**Impervious Cover Risk:** If the boundary dispute is resolved in favor of the adjacent property at 1501 Tulane St. — restoring the southern line to its historic, monumented position — the actual lot area of 1505 Tulane is reduced by an estimated 3 to 5 feet along a 132-foot run, potentially removing 400–660 sq ft from the denominator. The same 4,453 sq ft of impervious surface would then represent a higher percentage of a smaller lot. The Permitting Office should verify the impervious cover calculation against the true, surveyed lot area before issuing any permits.
</Warning>
