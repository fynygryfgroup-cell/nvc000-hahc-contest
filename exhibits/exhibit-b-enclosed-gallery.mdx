Exhibit B presents the proposed second floor plan (A01, Sheet 19), which is the critical evidentiary drawing disproving the applicant's claim that the garage is "detached" and connected only by an "unconditioned breezeway." The drawing reveals a fully enclosed, conditioned gallery with finished wood flooring, exterior walls, windows, interior doors, and an adjacent elevator shaft — constituting habitable, conditioned floor area that physically connects the main house second floor directly to the garage apartment.

<Warning>
**Continuous Conditioned Connection — Garage Is Attached:** The second floor plan shows an enclosed, conditioned **GALLERY (15'-5" × 4'-5")** with finished wood flooring, exterior walls, windows, and interior doors, plus an **ELEVATOR (5'-4" × 4'-2")** shaft, physically connecting the main house to the garage apartment. This is not an unconditioned breezeway. The garage is **structurally attached**, forfeiting the 528 sq ft detached garage FAR exemption. With the exemption removed, the project FAR rises to approximately **3,978 sq ft (45.2%)** — exceeding the strict 40% legal cap.
</Warning>

## Key Findings — Annotated Summary

The following findings are drawn directly from the face of the submitted drawing (A01, Sheet 19):

- **Gallery (15'-5" × 4'-5") — Enclosed, Conditioned, Finished:** The space labeled as a "breezeway" connection is a fully enclosed hallway with **finished wood flooring**, exterior walls, windows, and interior doors. Its dimensions (15'-5" × approximately 4'-5") confirm it is an enclosed, habitable passage — not an open, unconditioned breezeway.
- **Elevator Shaft (5'-4" × 4'-2") — Conditioned Infrastructure:** An elevator shaft measuring 5'-4" × 4'-2" is located adjacent to the gallery. This is conditioned, mechanically served infrastructure physically embedded in the connection between the main house and the garage apartment. Combined with the gallery, approximately **68 sq ft (gallery) + 22 sq ft (elevator) = ~90 sq ft** of connection space may be uncounted conditioned floor area not included in the declared FAR.
- **"25% ENCROACHMENT" Annotation:** A dashed line explicitly labeled **"25% ENCROACHMENT"** runs across the floor plan. The garage/breezeway area carries the annotation: *"GARAGE/BREEZEWAY FLOOR PLAN: 25% ENCROACHMENT NEW CONSTRUCTION APPLICATION."* This self-referential notation appears to acknowledge that the second-floor massing encroaches into a restricted area — likely the lot coverage or massing envelope limit. The HAHC must require the applicant to explain exactly what this encroachment line represents and how the proposed second floor complies with the Heights Design Guidelines when the drawing itself self-identifies as encroaching.
- **Continuous Conditioned Connection — Attached Determination:** The combination of the enclosed conditioned gallery, the elevator, and the continuous roofline documented in Exhibit C proves the garage is **structurally attached** to the main house. The 528 sq ft detached garage FAR exemption is therefore forfeited.

## Context: The "Detached Garage" Claim in the Submittal

The applicant's submittal narrative (A02, p. 1) states that the garage is "connected to the main house only by an unconditioned breezeway" and on this basis deducts **528 sq ft** from FAR and lot coverage calculations under the detached garage exemption. The second floor plan (A01, Sheet 19) directly contradicts this claim:

| Claimed by Applicant | Shown on Drawing |
|---|---|
| "Unconditioned breezeway" | Fully enclosed, conditioned GALLERY |
| No conditioned connection | Finished wood flooring, exterior walls, windows, interior doors |
| Detached structure | ELEVATOR shaft physically embedded in the connection |
| 528 sq ft FAR exemption valid | Exemption forfeited — structure is attached |
