Exhibit C presents the proposed roof plan (A01, Sheet 20), which provides the third and final confirmation — following the enclosed gallery (Exhibit B) and the plan dimensions (Exhibit A) — that the garage is structurally attached to the main house rather than detached. The roof plan shows a continuous roof plane tying the connector/breezeway roof directly into the exterior wall and lower eaves of the two-story addition, with no gap or structural separation joint. Under Houston Permitting standards, a continuous roofline converts a "detached" garage into an attached structure and forfeits the associated FAR exemption.

<Warning>
**Attached Structure Determination — FAR Exemption Forfeited:** The proposed roof plan (A01, Sheet 20) shows no gap or structural separation joint between the garage roof and the house roof. Under Houston Permitting standards, a **continuous roofline** connecting structures converts a "detached" garage into an **attached structure**. This forfeits the 528 sq ft detached garage FAR exemption, pushes the project FAR to approximately **3,978 sq ft (45.2%)** — exceeding the 40% legal cap — and means the garage cannot be treated as a separate accessory structure for setback, lot-line, or Chapter 42 purposes.
</Warning>

## Key Findings — Annotated Summary

The following findings are drawn directly from the face of the submitted drawing (A01, Sheet 20):

- **Continuous Roof Plane — Garage to Main House:** The garage and breezeway/connector are shown under a roof plane that connects directly to the main house addition. While the garage carries its own distinct hipped roof mass, the connector/breezeway roof ties directly into the exterior wall and lower eaves of the two-story addition without interruption.
- **No Structural Separation Joint:** There is no gap, expansion joint, or structural break shown between the garage roof and the house roof. The connection is continuous and uninterrupted across the full span from the garage through the breezeway/gallery connector to the main house addition.
- **Attached Structure Determination — Legal Consequence:** Under Houston Permitting standards, a continuous roofline and continuous foundation footings connecting structures convert a "detached" garage into an **attached structure**. The consequence is threefold:
  - The 528 sq ft detached garage FAR exemption is **forfeited**, pushing the project FAR to approximately 3,978 sq ft (45.2%) — over the 40% cap.
  - The garage **cannot be treated** as a separate accessory structure for setback purposes.
  - If the structure spans the interior lot line between Lots 16 and 17 without a recorded replat or binding agreement, it constitutes an **illegal building across an un-replatted lot line** under Chapter 42.

## Foundation Implication

The submittal packets do not include a dedicated foundation plan showing whether the garage and main house share a continuous slab or footing. However, the combination of (1) a continuous roofline as shown on A01, Sheet 20, (2) an enclosed conditioned gallery with an elevator connecting the second floors as shown on A01, Sheet 19, and (3) the absence of any structural separation joint in the full submittal packet strongly indicates the garage and house are being built as a single contiguous structure.

<Info>
The HAHC and Permitting Office should require the applicant to submit a dedicated foundation plan — currently absent from the submittal packets — before any determination is made regarding the attached vs. detached classification.
</Info>

## Chapter 42 Lot-Line Implication

If the garage is attached — connected by a continuous roofline and a conditioned interior gallery — and if Lots 16 and 17 have not been legally merged through a recorded Replat or Agreement to Bind Properties, then the attached structure spans across the interior lot line between two legally separate, un-replatted lots. This constitutes a direct violation of City of Houston Chapter 42 (Subdivisions & Development), which prohibits construction across un-replatted interior lot lines.

| Condition | Status | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Garage is attached (Exhibits B + C) | Confirmed on drawing face | 528 sq ft FAR exemption forfeited |
| Lots 16 & 17 recorded as one parcel | **No replat or binding agreement on record** | Structure spans un-replatted lot line — Chapter 42 violation |
| Garage treated as separate accessory structure | Invalid if attached | Cannot be evaluated independently for setback purposes |
