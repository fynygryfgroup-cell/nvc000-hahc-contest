> **Note on Directions:** The plat drawings for 1505 Tulane St. use a rotated orientation that does not match real-world cardinal directions. Throughout this document, directions are given as real-world direction (plat "X") — for example, "the north (plat "East") side setback" refers to the real-world north side of the property, which is labeled "East" on the plat drawings. The mapping is: plat "East" = real-world north; plat "South" = real-world east (Tulane Street); plat "West" = real-world south (1501 Tulane St. / boundary dispute side); plat "North" = real-world west (rear / building area).

**EXHIBIT A: Proposed Site Plan (A01, Sheet 17) — Setback Violations**

This exhibit shows the proposed site plan drawing with the actual dimensioned setbacks. The drawing itself reveals violations that contradict the narrative claims in the COA application.

Key findings visible on this drawing:

**NORTH (PLAT "EAST") SIDE SETBACK: 3'-6 1/2"** — The addition's north (plat "East") wall is drawn at 3'-6 1/2" from the north (plat "East") property line. This is 1'-5 1/2" below the mandatory 5' minimum side setback required under Chapter 33. The drawing even shows a "5' SIDE B.L." (building line) marker, and the wall is drawn inside it. This is a face-of-drawing violation, independent of the boundary dispute.

**WEST/REAR (PLAT "NORTH") SETBACK: 1'-0 1/8"** — The addition is drawn at 1'-0 1/8" from the west (plat "North") property line. The narrative claims "24' at least." This is a 23-foot discrepancy between the narrative and the drawing.

**GARAGE FOOTPRINT: 21'-1" × 22'-11 1/2"** — The drawing dimensions the garage at approximately 484 sq ft, but the narrative claims 680 sq ft. This is a 196 sq ft discrepancy.

**BREEZEWAY WIDTH: 8'-3"** — The gap between the garage and the main house addition.

**LOT DIMENSIONS: 66.66' × 132.00'** — Shown as Lots 16 & 17 combined, with no indication of the interior lot line.

**CUMULATIVE SIDE SETBACK: ~12'-0"** — The east side (3'-6 1/2") plus the west side (8'-5 15/16") total approximately 12 feet cumulative. The Houston Heights Design Guidelines require a minimum cumulative side setback of 15 feet for two-story houses [1]. This is a 3-foot deficiency — a face-of-drawing violation independent of the boundary dispute.

## Key Findings — Annotated Summary

The following findings are drawn directly from the face of the submitted drawing (A01, Sheet 17):

- **North (Plat "East") Side Setback — 3'-6½":** Wall drawn inside the "5' SIDE B.L." marker. Violation of the 5-foot minimum side setback under Chapter 33 by 1'-5½". This is a face-of-drawing violation that does not depend on the boundary dispute.
- **West/Rear (Plat "North") Setback — 1'-0⅛":** Addition drawn at 1'-0⅛" from the rear (plat "North") property line. The narrative claims "24' at least." A 23-foot discrepancy between the narrative text and the submitted drawing.
- **Garage Footprint Discrepancy — 196 sq ft:** Drawing dimensions yield 21'-1" × 22'-11½" ≈ **484 sq ft**. The submittal narrative claims a **680 sq ft** first-floor footprint. The 196 sq ft gap (approximately 40% more than the drawn dimensions support) calls into question either the accuracy of the drawing or the accuracy of the claimed square footage.
- **Breezeway Width — 8'-3":** The gap between the garage structure and the main house addition as dimensioned on the plan.
- **Lot Dimensions — 66.66' × 132.00':** Lots 16 and 17 are shown as a single combined parcel with no interior lot line depicted.
- **Cumulative Side Setback — ~12'-0" (3'-foot shortfall):** The north (plat "East") setback of 3'-6½" plus the south (plat "West") setback of 8'-5 15/16" yields approximately 12 feet cumulative. The Houston Heights Design Guidelines require a minimum cumulative total of **15 feet for two-story houses**. This is a 3-foot deficiency on the face of the submitted drawings.
